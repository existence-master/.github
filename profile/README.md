# Existence

Existence is a small company working on frontier technology. By frontier we mean the areas where the hard problems still are: artificial intelligence, robotics, space, energy, and whatever comes after them. We are not tied to one field. We pick problems that matter, that are not solved yet, and that we believe a small, serious team can move.

Right now our work is in AI. Our flagship is [Sentient](https://github.com/existence-master/sentient), a personal AI assistant that runs on your own computer. It handles long-running tasks, keeps real memory, helps before you ask, works by voice, and reaches your phone and smart glasses. Your data stays on your machine, and it runs on a local model or a cloud model of your choice.

Everything we build is open source, and we build it in public. The code, the issues, the plans and the mistakes are all here on GitHub. We think the technology that shapes the next few decades should be something people can read, run, change and own, not something they rent.

There is no application process. If you want to work with us, read the code, open an issue or send a pull request. Good ideas and good work are what count. If you would rather talk first, write to us at [existence.master@gmail.com](mailto:existence.master@gmail.com). The website is [existence.technology](https://existence.technology).
